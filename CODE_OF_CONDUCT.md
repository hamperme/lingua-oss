# Code of Conduct

Lingua OSS is intended to be a welcoming, technically rigorous open-source project.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence
- assume good faith while still asking for reproducible evidence
- make space for contributors with different levels of experience
- respect privacy, licensing, and attribution requirements
- avoid posting personal data, private transcripts, credentials, or other sensitive material
- accept maintainer decisions about scope when they are explained in project terms

## Unacceptable behavior

The project does not tolerate harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, sexualized conduct directed at other participants, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Technical disagreements

Disagreement is normal. Prefer small reproductions, tests, benchmarks, citations, and clearly stated trade-offs over personal arguments. Maintainers may close discussions that become repetitive or abusive while preserving the underlying technical issue when appropriate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and the project.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through a private GitHub channel rather than opening a public issue containing personal details.
